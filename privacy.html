Privacy Policy

BW Social Poster · Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what data BW Social Poster ("the App") handles and why. The App is used only by its account owner to publish their own content; we do not sell data or use it for advertising.

1. Data we access from TikTok

ScopeDataWhy
user.info.basicOpen ID, display name, avatarTo show which TikTok account you are posting to before you confirm a post.
video.publishCreator posting settings (privacy options, comment/Duet/Stitch settings, max video length) and the videos and captions you choose to publishTo publish videos you explicitly confirm, with the settings you select.

We do not read your followers, messages, analytics or any other TikTok data.

2. Other data

  • Access tokens for the platforms you connect (TikTok, Meta, YouTube, LINE).
  • Media files and captions you select for posting, and your posting schedule and history.
  • Images or captions sent to Google Gemini only when you click "suggest caption".

3. Where data is stored

  • On your own computer, in the App's data folder.
  • For scheduled posts that run while your computer is off: the media file, caption, schedule and access tokens are stored in our private cloud database and storage (Supabase), protected with access controls so that only our posting service can read them.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Media files uploaded to the cloud for TikTok and Instagram are deleted automatically right after they are published or if publishing fails.
  • Cancelling a scheduled post in the App deletes its cloud copy.
  • You can revoke the App's access at any time in each platform's settings (TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions). To request deletion of all data held in our cloud, contact us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

5. Sharing

Data is sent only to the platform you choose to post to (TikTok, Meta, YouTube, LINE), to Google Gemini when you request caption suggestions, and to our hosting provider (Supabase) to run scheduled posts. We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

6. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Cloud tables use row-level security and are not accessible with public keys. Access to the posting service requires a secret key held only by the App.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by each platform).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

9. Contact
